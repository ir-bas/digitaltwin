Press Conference: "Artificial Intelligence for Predicting Heart Disease"

On 27.03.2026, a press conference titled "Artificial Intelligence for Predicting Heart Disease" was held at the National Press Club of BTA in Veliko Tarnovo. The purpose of the press conference was to present the project funded by the Bulgarian National Science Fund, presented by the team of the Institute of Robotics – BAS. Journalists from various media outlets attended.
